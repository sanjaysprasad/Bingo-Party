How to Play Classic 1-25 BINGO

1. Fill Your Board: Fill your 5x5 grid with numbers 1 to 25. You can place them manually or tap Auto-Fill.

2. Take Turns: On your turn, tap any uncrossed number on your grid. That number is struck out on BOTH players' grids!

3. Complete Lines: A line is 5 crossed numbers in a horizontal row, vertical column, or diagonal line (12 possible lines).

4. Earn Letters:
• 1st Line = B is crossed out!
• 2nd Line = I is crossed out!
• 3rd Line = N is crossed out!
• 4th Line = G is crossed out!
• 5th Line = O is crossed out!

5. BINGO Win: The first player to complete 5 lines gets all B - I - N - G - O letters and wins the game!

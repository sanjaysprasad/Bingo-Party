📖 The Rules at a Glance

1. Fill 1 to 25: Arrange numbers 1 through 25 in any order on your 5x5 board, or click Auto-Fill.
2. Take Turns: On your turn, call any uncrossed number. It gets struck out on both players' grids!
3. Cross B-I-N-G-O: Each completed row, column, or diagonal line crosses out the next letter in B-I-N-G-O.
4. Win: First player to cross all 5 letters reaches BINGO and wins the match!

How to Play Classic 1-25 BINGO

1. Fill Your Board: Fill your 5x5 grid with numbers 1 to 25. You can place them manually or hit Auto-Fill / Shuffle.

2. Take Turns: On your turn, tap any uncrossed number on your grid to call it. That number is crossed out on BOTH players' grids!

3. Complete Lines: A line is 5 crossed numbers in a horizontal row, vertical column, or diagonal line (12 possible lines).

4. Earn Letters:
• 1st Line = B is crossed out!
• 2nd Line = I is crossed out!
• 3rd Line = N is crossed out!
• 4th Line = G is crossed out!
• 5th Line = O is crossed out!

5. BINGO Win: The first player to complete 5 lines gets all B - I - N - G - O letters and wins the game! Stats and dates are recorded in your profile.

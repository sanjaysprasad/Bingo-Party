How to Play Bingo Party

1. Join or Host: Host creates a room and sends the code or link to friends. Friends join on their phone or computer.

2. The Card: Each player gets a randomized 5x5 card. The center tile is a FREE space!

3. Calling Balls: The host draws balls (manually or auto-caller). Listen to the voice caller and watch the 3D ball tumbler.

4. Daubing: Tap matching numbers on your card to stamp them. Enable Auto-Daub if you want hands-free marking!

5. Claiming BINGO: When you complete the winning pattern, the giant golden ⚡ BINGO! ⚡ button unlocks. Tap it to claim victory and launch the confetti!
